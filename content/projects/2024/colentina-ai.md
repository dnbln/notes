[ColentinaAI](https://github.com/JetbrainsColentinaTei/ColentinaAI) (2024): An IntelliJ plugin that acts as an AI companion, and helps students solve programming questions.

Developed for the JetBrains Academy AIFest 2024 hackathon, in which it won 2nd place.
