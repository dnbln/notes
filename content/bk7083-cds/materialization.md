## Façades

For the façades we have created a parametric design in which we can very easily adjust all the single parts of the façade depending on its function. For instance, we can change the window's width depending on the sun-hours the building gets. On the North façade windows are 55% of the wall. This way lots of light can come in. We put the façade on an angle to accommodate the sound of the trains. The west (37%) and east (40%) façades have smaller windows to keep heat out as much as possible. For the south façade, windows take up 45% of the wall. Or we can change the angle of the solar panels depending on which side of the building they are on. We can adjust the depth of the balconies. Sound pollution is also taken into account when designing balconies. We have an inner and an outer layer for the balcony. These alternate per floor to allow light to come in. Finally, we have green roofs for the market hall and the main building. The rest of the market hall is made of glass to give it a spacious look and create a wide view.
